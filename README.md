# AI Meets Physics: My PINN Learning Journey 🚀

Welcome to my repository! This is the public log of my journey into the world of **Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)** and Computational Engineering.

Most machine learning models learn by finding patterns in massive datasets. But in engineering, we already know the rules—the laws of physics. The goal of this project is to explore how to build neural networks from scratch that don't just guess based on data, but actually understand and obey the laws of thermodynamics, mechanics, and material science.

## 🧠 What's in this repo?
This is just the beginning. I am currently focusing on building the core intuition and mathematical foundations without relying on heavy out-of-the-box physics libraries. 

Here are the concepts I am exploring and coding from the ground up:

* **Automatic Differentiation:** Using PyTorch's autograd engine to compute complex derivatives and understand how a neural network "feels" the math.
* **Heat Transfer:** Teaching a network how heat distributes across a 2D surface (Laplace and Poisson equations).
* **Solid Mechanics:** Simulating how a cantilever beam bends under physical load using neural networks instead of traditional finite element analysis.
* **Topology Optimization (TopOpt) Basics:** Exploring the early stages of making an AI design lightweight, structurally sound parts by manipulating density constraints.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
* **Python**
* **PyTorch** (The core engine for building the networks and calculating gradients)
* **Matplotlib** (For visualizing structural deformations and heat maps)

## 🚀 What's Next?
This is day one. The long-term vision is to move from these foundational 2D physics problems to advanced Deep Energy Methods, 3D simulations, and real-world engineering applications.
